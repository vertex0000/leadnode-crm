Data deletion

Last updated: 1 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the data Nodevers holds about you at any time.

If you have a Nodevers account

The workspace owner can remove people and data from inside the app. To delete your whole account and workspace, email shivamwaghmare4747@gmail.com from the email you signed up with. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

If a business messaged you through Nodevers

Email us with your phone number and the business name. We will pass the request to that business and delete the related messages and contact details within 30 days.

Data from Facebook / Meta login

If you connected Nodevers to Facebook, you can also remove it in Facebook → Settings → Apps and websites. Then email us and we will delete any data we received from Meta.
